# The Cheat Sheet

Everything on this site, compressed for retrieval.

## The anchor

*Generative sequence modelling is repeatedly predicting clean tokens from a
corrupted context. AR and diffusion differ only in the corruption process and
the decoding order.*

## The five equations

The AR factorization, exact by the chain rule:

$$
p(\mathbf{x}) = \prod_{i=1}^{L} p\left(x_i \mid x_{<i}\right)
$$

The masked forward process (linear schedule) and its objective, the weighted
masked cross-entropy that upper-bounds negative log-likelihood:

$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}_{t}\, w(t)\,
\mathbb{E}_{q}\!\!\sum_{i:\ x_t^i = \texttt{[M]}} -\log p_\theta\!\left(x_0^i \mid \mathbf{x}_t\right),
\qquad w(t) = \tfrac{1}{t}
$$

The any-order identity, the deepest fact in the subject:

$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}_{\sigma}\sum_{i}
-\log p_\theta\!\left(x_0^{\sigma(i)} \mid x_0^{\sigma(<i)}\right)
$$

The block bound, exact across blocks, bound within, AR at $B{=}1$:

$$
\log p_\theta(\mathbf{x}) \ge \sum_{b} -\mathcal{L}_{\text{masked}}\!\left(\mathbf{x}^{(b)} \mid \mathbf{x}^{(<b)}\right)
$$

Conditional flow matching, the formalism that contains all of it (discrete
case: velocity becomes a CTMC rate matrix; mask source recovers masked
diffusion):

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CFM}} = \mathbb{E}\left\lVert v_\theta(\mathbf{x}_t, t) - u_t(\mathbf{x}_t \mid \mathbf{x}_1) \right\rVert^2
$$

## The four axes

**State space** (absorbing / uniform / continuous) · **granularity** (canvas /
blocks) · **provenance** (scratch / adapted) · **decoding policy** (random /
confidence / semi-AR / distilled). Cache, length cap, and likelihood tightness
are consequences of granularity; self-correction of state space plus policy;
training cost of provenance.

## Decide in one breath

Exact likelihood or long cached context → **AR**. Infilling or bidirectional
structure → **masked**. Serving speed or unbounded length → **block**. Small
budget → **adapt**. Gradient steering → **continuous**. Few-step sampling →
**uniform, distilled**. Likelihood with parallelism → **hybrid**.

## Glossary, ten terms

**NFE**: number of function evaluations, the denoising step count $N$; the
compute-quality dial. **NELBO**: negative evidence lower bound; what a dLLM
reports instead of an exact NLL, always an upper bound. **Absorbing kernel**:
corruption where tokens become `[M]` and stay there; makes corruption
observable. **Any-order AR**: the identity showing masked diffusion equals AR
averaged over generation orders. **Canvas**: the fixed-length sequence a
full-granularity dLLM denoises. **Block-causal attention**: bidirectional
within a block, causal across blocks; what makes the block cache exact.
**Confidence unmasking**: committing the most peaked predictions first.
**Remasking**: re-corrupting a committed token to revise it; no AR analogue.
**Generative perplexity**: sample quality scored by a judge LM; gameable by
low-entropy samplers, never report alone. **Multimodality problem**: the
within-step independence error, named in 2018, visible at NFE 1.
